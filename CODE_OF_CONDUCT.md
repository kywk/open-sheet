# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## What is expected

- Welcome newcomers, and remember that a beginner's question is not a burden
- Take disagreement about technical decisions as normal and useful
- Accept a decision once it is made, even when you argued the other way
- Give feedback that names the problem and, where you can, a way forward

## What is not

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainer through a private GitHub message or the
security advisory form. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not fit this document, and may
ban repeat or serious offenders.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.
